What this toy model includes
  • Detector: 78 strings 125 m apart with 60 DOMs each, plus 8 denser DeepCore strings. Positions are approximate.
  • Light yield: about 170,000 Cherenkov photons per GeV of shower energy. Hadronic showers give 85% of that.
  • Muon energy loss: dE/dx = a + bE, with a = 0.24 GeV/m and b = 3.3 × 10⁻⁴ per metre. E is the muon energy and x is the distance travelled.
  • Tau decay length: 49 m per PeV of tau energy on average.
  • Ice: light fades as exp(−r/λ)/(4πr²), where r is the distance and λ is 30 m in clear ice and 12 m in the dust layer near 2,050 m depth.
  • Charge is in photoelectrons (PE), the number of photons a DOM detects. Sensor size is tuned to give about 0.2 PE per GeV when averaged over interaction points. Events close to a string give more.
  • Not included: Cherenkov cone direction, real ice layers and tilt, noise hits, PMT saturation, Glashow resonance.
