IceCube Neutrino Event Simulator

A neutrino hits the ice, charged particles make blue Cherenkov light, and 5,160 sensors (DOMs) record it. Change the neutrino and watch the light pattern change.

Signature events
Neutrino

Log scale, from 1 TeV to 10 PeV. CC makes a charged lepton. NC makes only a shower. GR is an electron antineutrino hitting an electron.

Arrival direction

Zenith 0° comes straight down from the sky. Zenith 180° comes up through the Earth.

Interaction point

The instrumented ice spans about 600 m from the centre and 1,450 to 2,450 m in depth. Points beyond that are outside the detector.

What this toy model includes
  • Detector: 78 strings 125 m apart with 60 DOMs each, plus 8 denser DeepCore strings. Positions are approximate.
  • Light yield: about 170,000 Cherenkov photons per GeV of shower energy. Hadronic showers give 85% of that.
  • Muon energy loss: dE/dx = a + bE, with a = 0.24 GeV/m and b = 3.3 × 10⁻⁴ per metre. A tau uses b = 5 × 10⁻⁵ per metre, so a tau track is fainter than a muon track.
  • Tau decay length: 49 m per PeV of tau energy on average. Decays: 17% muon, 18% electron, 65% hadrons.
  • Kinematics: x and y are drawn from simple toy shapes. Q² is capped near the W boson mass squared, which pushes x down at high energy.
  • Glashow resonance: the W boson decays to hadrons 67% of the time, otherwise to an electron, muon or tau with a neutrino.
  • Ice: light fades as exp(−r/λ)/(4πr²), where r is the distance and λ is 30 m in clear ice and 12 m in the dust layer near 2,050 m depth.
  • Charge is in photoelectrons (PE), the number of photons a DOM detects. Sensor size is tuned to give about 0.2 PE per GeV when averaged over interaction points.
  • Not included: Cherenkov cone direction, real ice layers and tilt, noise hits, PMT saturation, tau energy loss before decay.
